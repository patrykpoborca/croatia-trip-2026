Croatia + Herzegovina • October 9–19

Adriatic, canyons, islands & old cities

A review deck for a couples trip built around adventure, natural wonders, culture, and a little luxury—without over-cramming mid-October travel.

Route: Zagreb → Plitvice → Krka → Split/Omiš → Hvar → Dubrovnik → Mostar Flights: into Zagreb (LH 1404, Oct 10 14:00), out of Dubrovnik Style: active, scenic, flexible
Route logic

Compact geography, high variety

This route avoids the classic mistake of trying to include Slovenia, Montenegro, Bosnia, every island, and every waterfall park in one short trip.

ZagrebLanding night: Upper Town, Stone Gate, štrukli. Car picked up at the airport.
Plitvice + KrkaRastoke & Barać on the rainy day, Plitvice on the dry one, Krka on the drive south.
SplitRoman palace core in the evenings and the Oct 14 morning.
OmišCetina canyon rafting, zipline, canyoning, fort hikes.
HvarIsland reset: coastline, villages, wine, Fortica, optional boat/kayak.
DubrovnikWalls, Srđ, Lokrum, kayak, old city atmosphere.
MostarHerzegovina culture shift with Kravica, Počitelj, Blagaj options.

Things to lock in

Logistics checklist

Car

Rental: Zagreb airport Oct 10 → Split Oct 13 (one-way)

Pick up on landing at Zagreb (expect a one-way drop fee). Keep it through Omiš and Klis on Oct 13, then return it in Split that evening — so the Split stay needs parking for the night of Oct 12 (or use a public garage on the Old Town edge). Rent a second car in Dubrovnik just for Oct 18 — or book a private driver for Mostar (no border paperwork, ~€300–400 for two). If self-driving into Bosnia, ask the rental company for the cross-border "green card".

Ferries

Split → Hvar, Hvar → Dubrovnik

Checked on Ferryhopper Oct 10: Oct 14 Split → Hvar has 9 sailings (13:00 Krilo is the pick); Oct 16 Hvar → Dubrovnik has 3 (09:10 Krilo, 10:40 and 16:45 TP Line) — book the 09:10 now. Times, prices and booking links are in Boats & Rentals.

Timed tickets

Plitvice & Dubrovnik Walls

Plitvice sells timed-entry tickets online — book Oct 11 around 11:00 (after Rastoke). Walls tickets are bought on the day; the Dubrovnik Pass bundles walls + Lovrijenac + museums + buses.

Season

Shoulder-season closures

Mid-October is the tail of the season: some Hvar hotels, beach bars and Pakleni taxi boats wind down around Oct 15–20. Cetina rafting and Omiš zipline usually run through October but confirm. Sea ~20 °C — swimming is still possible on sunny days.

Walk-in on the morning works in October. A 50 cc scooter needs only a car licence; 125 cc may need a motorcycle licence, so ask. Bring your licence plus the International Driving Permit.

Bus instead

Čazmatrans island bus

Hvar Town ⇄ Stari Grad in ~20 min, with connections to Jelsa and Vrboska. Only a few runs a day, cash to the driver (~€2–4). The off-season timetable is thin, so check before relying on it.

Čazmatrans timetable ↗ · Guide: Hvar Town → Stari Grad without a car ↗

Bike instead

E-bike the Stari Grad Plain

Take the bus or a taxi to Stari Grad, rent a bike there (~€13–15 a day), and ride the flat 2,400-year-old Greek field grid. Grab the free map at the tourist office. Guided e-bike tours run from Hvar Town.

Sat Oct 10 • Sleep Zagreb

Zagreb: land, pick up the car, Upper Town evening

Goal: easy first evening after an overnight flight
✨ Browse this day's activities →✈️ LH 1404 Frankfurt 12:30 → Zagreb 14:00🚗 Airport → centre · 25 min🌧️ Forecast: rain, ~15 °C
Primary

Upper Town loop

Ban Jelačić Square → cathedral → Stone Gate → St. Mark's Church → Lotrščak Tower and the funicular down. About 2 h at an easy pace.

Rain-proof

Museum of Broken Relationships

Zagreb's most original museum, open 9–21 in October. Next to St. Mark's.

Dinner

Štrukli + Tkalčićeva

Štrukli at La Štruk, then drinks on Tkalčićeva. Dolac market closes ~15:00 on Saturdays, so do it Sunday morning instead.

High energyUpper Town loop + museum + Tkalčićeva night.
Medium energyUpper Town loop, štrukli dinner.
Low energyCheck in, dinner near the square, early sleep.
Sun Oct 11 • Sleep Plitvice area

Rastoke in the morning, Plitvice all afternoon

Goal: both lake areas without rushing
✨ Browse this day's activities →🚗 Zagreb → Rastoke · 1 h 20🚗 Rastoke → Plitvice · 30 min🎟️ Timed entry ~11:00🌦️ Rain overnight, dry by midday
8:50–9:50

Rastoke

Loop walk among the water mills and cascades (~€5), the working 18th-century mill, coffee over the falls at Vodena Ovca, and bread from Pod Rastočkim Krovom for lunch in the park.

~11:00–16:00

Plitvice: Program C

Walk the Upper Lakes first while they're quieter, take the Kozjak boat, then the Lower Lakes and Veliki Slap facing the falls. Expect slippery boardwalks after the rain and strong waterfalls.

Evening

Lika dinner

Lamb under the bell at Lička kuća by Entrance 1, or the Rastovača home cooking class.

If it's pouringSwap the park afternoon for Barać Caves and walk Plitvice at 8:00 on Oct 12 instead (Krka then moves to the afternoon).
If you'd rather sleep inSkip Rastoke and leave Zagreb at 8:30.

Krka: Skradinski Buk

Tue Oct 13 • Sleep Split

Omiš & Cetina Canyon → Klis at sunset

Goal: make this the highest-adrenaline day
✨ Browse this day's activities →🚗 Split → Omiš · 41 min / 25 km🚣 Rafting · 3–4 h door-to-door🚗 Omiš → Klis · ~40 min → Split · 20 min🔑 Return the car in Split tonight
🚣Adventure

Cetina rafting

Most reliable adventure format: scenic canyon, rapids, swimming/calm sections, half-day footprint.

🧗Adventure

Canyoning

Most memorable if operating: swimming, scrambling, jumps/rappels depending on level.

🪂Adventure

Zipline

Great pairing with rafting; high canyon views without technical skill.

🏰Hike

Fortica / Mirabela

Mirabela is easy from town; Fortica is the better real hike and viewpoint.

Evening

Klis Fortress at sunset (~18:20), then Split

Klis was the Oct 12 pick in the old plan; it now caps this day on the drive back. Spit-roast lamb at Perlica, or the Split Old Town food tour.

Weather / season backup

Biokovo Nature Park + Skywalk

If river operators are closed or conditions are poor, convert the day into a mountain viewpoint/nature day. This is the strongest true substitute for Omiš.

Split's palace

Sat Oct 17 • Sleep Dubrovnik

Dubrovnik: walls, sea and island options

Goal: culture plus active coastline
✨ Browse this day's activities →🚶 Walls loop · 1.5–2 h⛴️ Old Port → Lokrum · 15 min🚗 Dubrovnik → Trsteno · 25 min
Primary morning

City Walls + Fort Lovrijenac

Do this first while fresh. The wall circuit is the single attraction in Dubrovnik most worth protecting from fatigue or bad timing.

Primary afternoon

Lokrum or sea kayak

Lokrum gives forest, monastery ruins, botanical gardens and a tiny salt lake; kayak is the more active version if sea conditions are excellent.

Windy-sea alternateTrsteno Arboretum by land.
Rain alternateRector's Palace, Maritime Museum, churches, cafés.

Sun Oct 18 • Sleep Dubrovnik

Herzegovina day: Mostar + waterfalls

Goal: cultural contrast from the Adriatic coast
✨ Browse this day's activities →🚗 Dubrovnik → Kravica → Počitelj → Mostar · 3 h 33 min / 180 km🚗 Mostar → Dubrovnik · 2 h 39 min🛂 2 border crossings — bring passports
Primary

Kravica Falls

Nature opener; wide waterfall amphitheater. Swim only if weather/water temperature cooperate.

Primary

Počitelj

Fortified Ottoman hillside village with quick climb and Neretva views.

Primary

Mostar

Stari Most, bazaar, riverbanks, mosque viewpoint, Bosnian coffee and food.

Optional

Blagaj

Buna spring + dervish monastery; strong but squeezes Mostar time if done on a group schedule.

Decision rule

Mostar > Kravica > Počitelj ≈ Blagaj

With a private driver and smooth borders, do all four. With a group tour or delays, protect Mostar first, then Kravica.

Mon Oct 19 · Getting home on standby

Flights to United hubs

Every nonstop flight on Oct 19 from Dubrovnik, Split and Zagreb to a European hub United flies from, all under 3 h. Prices are the cheapest one-way economy fare per person on Google Flights (checked Oct 10, USD; usually hand-baggage only). ☀️ Same day = lands by ~10:45, early enough to try for a late-morning United departure. 🌙 Overnight = sleep at the hub and fly standby on Oct 20. Check United's actual Oct 19 departure times and loads before choosing.

Best same-day setup

DBV → Frankfurt 07:30

Lands 09:30, ~2.5 h before typical United departures, at the gateway you already flew through. Croatia Airlines / Lufthansa, $321.

Bargain

DBV → Vienna 08:40

$51 on Ryanair (Lauda), lands 10:05. Only worth it if United's Vienna flight has good standby odds that day. Small personal item only.

If loads look bad

Evening → hub hotel

DBV → Munich 17:05 ($247) or → Frankfurt 18:55 ($265), sleep at the hub, then the first United flight on Oct 20. Gives you a last morning in Dubrovnik or Cavtat.
